Privacy Notice

For the IntellEducare website and enquiry form

This notice explains how information submitted through the IntellEducare website enquiry form is intended to be used. Please review it before submitting an enquiry.

Information you may provide

The form may ask for a student or parent name, mobile number, email address, standard, board or learning pathway, preferred area, and a message. Please do not submit passwords, payment information, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information through the form.

How we use enquiry details

The details are used to receive, understand and respond to your enquiry; discuss potentially relevant programs; answer questions about current availability; and follow up on the request you submitted. This form is not designed to automatically subscribe you to marketing communications.

Service providers and delivery

When the online enquiry service is enabled, a hosting provider processes the website request and an email-delivery provider (configured by the website operator) is used to deliver the enquiry to Info@intelleducare.com. Those providers may process technical information required to operate, secure and deliver the service. Their own privacy notices and retention terms also apply.

Retention and security

Enquiry information should be retained only for as long as needed to respond, maintain appropriate records or meet applicable legal requirements. The website uses transport security on its production host and server-side handling for the email API key. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please share only the information needed to answer your question.

Cookies and analytics

This website starter does not intentionally install advertising cookies or run third-party analytics. Essential technical logs may be generated by the hosting or delivery providers. If analytics, embedded services, advertising tools or additional integrations are added later, this notice should be updated before they are enabled.

Your requests and contact

For a request about enquiry information you have provided, contact Info@intelleducare.com. The website operator should verify your request as appropriate and respond in accordance with applicable law.

Before public launch: IntellEducare should review this notice against its actual data handling, hosting, email-provider settings, retention practices and applicable Indian privacy requirements. This starter wording is not legal advice.

We may revise this notice when the website or its data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.
